Question

How did members of Congress trade stocks as COVID-19 moved from a distant outbreak to a U.S. market shock, and how did those trades perform over the following year?

Data and methods

This March 25, 2025 Queens College project used Quiver Quantitative’s politician trading dataset: 48,422 congressional trades from 2012 to 2024. The study isolated 2,528 trades placed between December 30, 2019 and March 31, 2020, including 70 trades in medical and COVID-related stocks.

The analysis was written in R with tidyverse, ggplot2, and quantmod.

Findings

  • Medical-stock purchases in that window averaged a 21.4% one-year gain.
  • The 10 most-purchased stocks in the window averaged a 41.4% one-year gain. Microsoft (MSFT) was the most traded name, with 54 trades.
  • The write-up cross-checked notable traders including Loeffler, Cisneros, and Curtis. Those trades were flagged for review.
